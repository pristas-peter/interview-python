Five example loan applications. Use these to test your service.

These are in plain prose on purpose — designing the actual request/response
schema (field names, types, structure) is part of the exercise. Translate
each example into your own JSON (or whatever you prefer).

Expected outcomes are intentionally not provided — derive them from the spec.

================================================================================
Example 1 — Clean approve
================================================================================

A 28-year-old software engineer applies for a used-car loan. They earn
4,000 EUR per month, have 200 EUR of existing monthly debt, and want to
borrow 15,000 EUR over 36 months. Their stated reason: "Buying a used car
to commute to a new job."

================================================================================
Example 2 — High amount, otherwise healthy
================================================================================

A dentist earning 12,000 EUR per month with 1,000 EUR of existing monthly
debt applies for a 250,000 EUR loan over 60 months to purchase a family
home with their partner.

================================================================================
Example 3 — High debt-to-income
================================================================================

A barista earning 2,000 EUR per month, already carrying 800 EUR of monthly
debt, applies for 30,000 EUR over 36 months. They say they need to replace
a broken refrigerator and a couple of other appliances.

================================================================================
Example 4 — Blocked occupation
================================================================================

An applicant lists their occupation as "professional gambler". They earn
5,000 EUR per month, have 500 EUR of existing debt, and want 20,000 EUR
over 48 months for renovating their apartment.

================================================================================
Example 5 — LLM red flag on otherwise clean inputs
================================================================================

A graphic designer earning 3,500 EUR per month, with 300 EUR of monthly
debt, applies for 8,000 EUR over 24 months. Their stated reason: "I want
to put it all on red at the roulette table next weekend at a casino in
Monaco."
